3. Android Device Permissions & Strict Safeguards

To provide mindful pause overlays and screen-time insights, AppBrake requests specific Android permissions. We use these permissions strictly for their stated on-device digital wellbeing functions:

Accessibility Service (BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE)

Used solely to detect when a user launches a protected app in real-time and display the intentional pause screen before access is granted. We do NOT monitor keystrokes, read on-screen text, inspect passwords, capture sensitive personal data, or read messages.

Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS)

Allows AppBrake to calculate your daily app open counts, screen time insights, and display accurate resistance statistics on your device.

Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)

Enables the AppBrake mindful pause screen to open seamlessly on top of a locked application before access is granted.

Foreground Service & Watchdog (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE)

Ensures the background monitoring watchdog remains active to detect app launches reliably, displaying persistent active status as mandated by Android OS.

Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

Required on Android 13 (API 33) and above to display the active monitoring status and alert you when a temporary access window is expiring.

Boot & Battery Optimization

RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED restarts protection after your device reboots. REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS prevents aggressive OEM battery savers from terminating the pause watchdog.
